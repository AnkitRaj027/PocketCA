📱 Pocket CA Chatbot – Your Chartered Accountant in Your Pocket
Pocket CA is a smart, interactive chatbot designed to simplify financial and accounting tasks for individuals and businesses. Acting like a digital Chartered Accountant, it helps you manage expenses, track income, prepare financial documents, and answer queries about taxation, compliance, and investments—all in real time.

✨ Key Features
Expense Tracking: Record and categorize daily transactions effortlessly.

Tax Guidance: Get quick answers on filing, deductions, and compliance.

Financial Planning: Receive insights on budgeting, savings, and investments.

Document Support: Generate schedules, summaries, and reports for loans or audits.

24/7 Availability: Always ready to assist, right from your phone or laptop.
